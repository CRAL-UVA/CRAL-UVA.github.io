  <h2>Humanoid Robot Autonomy</h2>
</div>

<div class="rl-lede" markdown="0">
<p> It's an exciting time to be doing research in humanoid robotics. The following are some of the open problems CRΔL is focusing on:</p>

<ol class="rl-open">
  <li><span class="rl-q">Human-Robot-Environment Co-optimization (HERO).</span> While necessary as a key component, physical AI alone is insufficient for autonomous humanoid robots, particularly when the environment is unstructured, messy, and complex. Going forwards, we will need to co-optimize the robot&rsquo;s environment in parallel with its autonomy.</li>
  <li><span class="rl-q">Integration of reasoning and control in a closed-loop feedback in open worlds.</span> Without integration, robots can either reason without acting (like a chatbot) or act without reasoning (factory, warehouse robots). Without closed-loop feedback, we are restricted to &ldquo;impressive&rdquo;, but otherwise less-than-useful, open-loop rollouts (dancing, parkour etc.). And without the open world constraint, robots will never move out of the lab.</li>
  <li><span class="rl-q">Solving SMGs with humanoid robots.</span> SMGs with wheeled robots are much simpler since the action space is two-dimensional. Humanoid robots are not only high-dimensional, but also underactuated. Therefore, in addition to motion planning, one must also solve whole body control problems that arise in an SMG.</li>

</ol>
</div>
